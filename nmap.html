What is Nmap?

Nmap is a tool used to discover devices on a network and identify open ports and services running on those devices.

What are Ports?

Ports are communication channels used by services. Open ports can reveal what software is running and may expose vulnerabilities.

Example Command

nmap -sV 192.168.1.0/24

This scans an entire network and identifies service versions, helping ethical hackers detect outdated or insecure software.
