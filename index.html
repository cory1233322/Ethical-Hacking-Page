What is Ethical Hacking?

Ethical hacking is the practice of testing computer systems, networks, and applications to find security weaknesses before attackers can exploit them. It is performed with full permission from the organization.

Ethical vs Illegal Hacking

Both ethical and illegal hackers use similar tools and techniques. The difference is authorization. Ethical hackers are hired and allowed to test systems, while illegal hackers act without permission and with harmful intent.

Why Companies Hire Ethical Hackers

Organizations hire ethical hackers to simulate real attacks, identify vulnerabilities, and improve security systems. They help prevent data breaches, financial loss, and system damage.
